Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 14, 2026

CupidPout (“we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how the CupidPout mobile app handles information when you use it.

Photos and camera

The app may request access to your camera so you can take a photo, and permission to add an edited result to your photo library. Photos you select or create are used to provide the editing features on your device.

On-device face analysis and photo retention

To create a preview, CupidPout analyzes facial landmarks in the selected photo on your device. Neither the selected photo nor facial-landmark data is uploaded to us or to our servers. We do not operate a server for storing or viewing your photos or facial-landmark data.

The app does not create a separate copy of your original photo. Photo and landmark information used for an edit is kept only in working memory while you use the editing feature and is discarded when it is no longer needed. An edited result is saved to your photo library only when you choose to save it. You can delete any saved result at any time in the Apple Photos app; after deletion, we have no copy to retain or delete.

Advertising

The free version of CupidPout may show ads provided by Google Mobile Ads and its advertising partners. These providers may process device and online identifiers, approximate location, and information about ad interactions to deliver, measure, and improve advertising, subject to your choices and their policies.

Where required, the app presents a consent flow and offers an in-app privacy-options entry so you can review or change advertising choices. You can also manage relevant permissions and advertising settings through your device settings.

Purchases

If you choose to buy CupidPout Pro, the transaction is processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment-card details.

Information stored on your device

The app stores settings and usage information locally on your device, such as language, appearance preferences, and available free-edit usage. This information is used to make the app work and is not sent to us by the app.

Children

CupidPout is not directed to children under the age at which they can consent to data processing in their country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last revised.

Contact

For privacy questions, please contact us through the CupidPout listing on the App Store.
